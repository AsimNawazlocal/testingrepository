====================================================================
Primary source: Final_50_City_Master_Dataset.xlsx, Sandy row (Launch

  misattribution pattern seen in other cities in this portfolio.
  These were excluded from on-page content and from any

- Soil claim correction: the dataset's "caliche" soil risk-factor

  search and replaced on the Slab Leak page with locally accurate
  "clay-heavy, expansion-prone soil" language for the Salt Lake
  Valley.

  was checked against Rheem's published range (15–20 years typical,
  25–30 well-maintained) and softened to "15–25 years" on the FAQs
  page to avoid repeating an unverified upper bound.
- Cost Pages sheet (9 usable Sandy rows; Sump Pump pre-excluded in
  the sheet) was cross-checked against independent sources before
  the /cost/ page shipped: HomeServe's Utah-specific water heater
  data ($1,537 Utah average, ~10% above the $1,396 national average)
  supports the dataset's $1,200–$2,800 tank-replacement range;
  HomeAdvisor/Bob Vila whole-house PEX repiping figures and
  Angi/thepricer/Fixr CIPP lining per-linear-foot data ($80–$250/ft)
  were checked against the repiping and trenchless-lining ranges. All
  9 dataset ranges held up as reasonable and were kept unchanged.
- Verified real local specifics used across the site (not
  fabricated): Old Mill/Cottonwood Paper Mill ruins and the Big
  Cottonwood Canyon mouth (Cottonwood Heights); Dimple Dell Regional
  Park and Alta Canyon Drive (Sandy Hills); America First Field at
  9256 S State St (confirmed as the real Salt Lake MLS stadium, not a
  contamination artifact); White City's 1955 founding by M. Kenneth
  White, its May 1 2024 incorporation, and its own separate water
  utility (White City Water Improvement District) — confirmed via
  real street-level address data for Pepperwood Dr/Pt, Hidden Valley
  Dr/Blvd/Rd/Club Dr, and Willow Creek Dr/Estates.
- Business address, GPS coordinates, and phone (placeholder) pulled
  from the dataset's Master Overview sheet, used as-is (verified

4. PER-PAGE WORD COUNTS
====================================================================
See page-inventory.csv for the full list (34 URLs). Summary:

- 19 service pages + 6 area/location pages: all verified to contain
  1,000+ meaningful words in rendered <main>, per the master prompt's
  explicit Section 1 requirement. Range: ~1,005–1,093 words.
- IMPORTANT CORRECTION: during final-deliverables prep, a defect was
  found in the word-counting method used throughout this build. The
  function stripped known non-content divs/figures/comments but never
  removed the page's embedded <style> block before counting words,
  so every per-batch count reported and approved earlier in this
  build (e.g. "water heater installation 1080," "tankless 1031," all
  6 area-page counts) was inflated by roughly 300–350 words of raw
  CSS property text being counted as content. Once corrected (CSS and
  script blocks stripped, count restricted to rendered <main> only,
  matching the master prompt's literal wording), every one of the 25
  service/area pages was actually below the 1,000-word floor — true
  counts ranged ~667–757 words before the fix.
  This was caught and fixed before delivery: 2–3 new genuine,
  non-filler <h2> sections were added to each of the 25 pages (real
  troubleshooting detail, process explanation, or area-specific
  operational detail — never padding or repetition), written to
  extend each page's already-established scope without duplicating
  its own existing headings or another page's anti-cannibalization
  boundary. All 25 pages were re-verified after the fix using the
  corrected method and now genuinely clear the floor. A full
  duplicate-heading-sequence check and a forbidden-phrase/em-dash/
  form/excluded-service resweep were both re-run after the fix and
  came back clean (2 new automated hits, both verified false
  positives: "cheaper" as a cost comparison, "wax-free" as a compound
  word).
- Supporting pages (FAQs, Cost, Services hub, Areas hub, Contact,
  About, Privacy, 404) have no word floor per Section 1 (explicitly
  scoped to service/area pages only) and range from 33 words (404) to
  1,281 words (Cost).
- Homepage: 1,485 words (no floor applies, built in an earlier
  session).
