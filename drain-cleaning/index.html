Drain Cleaning & Clog Removal: What's Included

Most kitchen and bathroom clogs in Grapevine are grease, hair, and soap scum sitting in one branch line, and a single cable run settles those. The harder calls come back within a few months of that first cable service. That pattern almost always means the pipe wall itself has scaled down with mineral deposits from the area's hard water, narrowing the usable diameter enough that even a clear line clogs again fast. A cable tunnels a hole through that buildup. It does not remove it.

Hydro jetting is the fix: high-pressure water scours the full diameter of the pipe clean instead of punching a path through the middle. It is also the right call for storm drains, French drains, and commercial kitchen lines carrying grease loads a household drain never sees. A drain camera usually runs first on any repeat-clog call, so the choice between cable and jetting comes from what the pipe actually looks like inside, not a guess.

A cleanout overflowing in the yard, not backing up inside the house, usually means the blockage sits in the main lateral between the house and the street, not in any single branch. Still a drain-clearing call, just one that starts from the outside cleanout instead of a fixture inside.

Kitchen lines carrying grease behave differently from a bathroom clog. Grease cools and hardens against the pipe wall as it travels away from the sink, narrowing the line gradually rather than blocking it outright. A kitchen drain that empties slowly for weeks before finally stopping is a classic grease pattern, not a sudden foreign-object clog. A cable can break through it, but jetting clears the coating itself, which is why repeat kitchen-line calls usually end with a jetting recommendation instead of another cable pass.

A standard single-fixture cable service usually runs $150 to $300 and finishes within an hour. A camera inspection added to that visit runs somewhat more, and a full jetting pass on a main lateral runs higher still, closer to the range quoted on the hydro jetting page itself, since it is a longer job with more equipment involved. The exact number depends on how far the clog sits from the nearest access point.

Why This Looks Different Here

Water hardness across this part of Tarrant County is high enough that scale inside drain lines is a routine finding on older homes, not a rare one. Houses built before the mid-1990s, concentrated closer to Historic Main Street, see this most because their cast iron and early PVC lines have had more decades for mineral content to build up.

Mature tree cover is a second local factor. Grapevine's older streets, planted up through the 1980s, have root systems that have had decades to find their way into the small cracks and joints of a clay or early PVC sewer lateral. A slow drain that only backs up after a heavy watering is a common tell for root intrusion, not ordinary grease and soap buildup.

The Visit, Step by Step

We start by asking where the slow drain is and what else connects to that same branch, since a single clogged fixture and a clogged shared line present differently. A cable usually runs first on a standard clog. A camera inspection gets added if the clog repeats, the line is a main lateral, or the cable comes back with scale instead of a clean removal, so the choice between cable and jetting comes from what the camera shows. Most single-fixture clogs clear within the hour. A full lateral jetting job with a camera pass before and after takes longer and gets scheduled for that.

When a Different Page Is the Right One

A main-lateral blockage that keeps recurring even after jetting, or one with a cracked or offset section of pipe visible on camera, moves to Sewer Line Repair & Replacement. A single fixture that is slow but not fully blocked, a shower valve dripping rather than draining slow, is usually a Faucet & Sink issue, not a drain-clearing one.

A Common Question Here

How often should residential drains be professionally cleaned or hydro jetter cleared?

Standard preventative drain cleaning is recommended every 18 to 24 months for single-family homes. For properties with mature tree roots or frequent kitchen grease disposal, annual hydro jetting maintains optimal pipe flow and prevents emergency sewer backups.
