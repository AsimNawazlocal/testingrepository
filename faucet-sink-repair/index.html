Faucet & Sink Repair in Grapevine, TX

A dripping faucet, a leaking shower valve, a cracked outdoor hose bib, or a new sink and fixture set installed cleanly, all fixture-level work handled in a single visit.

What This Service Includes

A dripping faucet is almost always a worn cartridge, washer, or O-ring. Which one depends on the faucet's internal design, compression, ball, cartridge, or ceramic disc, and each fails differently with its own replacement part. A faucet dripping steadily from the spout points to one of those internal seals. One leaking at the base while running points to a different O-ring entirely. Getting that right up front avoids a return trip for the wrong part.

Shower valves and bathtub spouts follow a similar pattern but with higher pressure and more moving parts behind the wall, which is why a leaking shower valve sometimes means opening a small access panel rather than working from the showerhead side alone. A valve that starts mixing hot and cold incorrectly, not just leaking, usually means the cartridge itself needs replacing, not a simple reseal.

Outdoor hose bibs carry their own seasonal failure pattern here. A bib that was not drained, or that lacked a frost-proof design, before a hard freeze can crack internally with no visible damage until the following spring, when it leaks inside the wall it is mounted on. The standard fix after a freeze-related failure is a frost-proof hose bib, which shuts the water off well back inside the warm part of the wall instead of right at the spigot.

Sink and faucet installation, including a kitchen remodel or a simple upgrade, gets scoped against the existing supply connections and drain configuration. A new faucet or sink does not always match the rough-in of the one it replaces, especially on an older home where fixture spacing has shifted over the decades.

A bathroom sink's pop-up drain assembly and a kitchen sink's basket strainer are small, often-overlooked parts of a sink call. Both fail in similarly minor but annoying ways: a pop-up that will not stay closed, a basket strainer leaking slowly at its mounting gasket. Neither is a big job, and both commonly get bundled into the same visit as a faucet repair since they tend to get noticed together.

Most single-fixture repairs, a cartridge swap, a hose bib replacement, a pop-up assembly, run under two hours and under $300 including the part, with parts for the common brands carried on the truck. A full faucet or sink installation, including removing the old fixture, usually takes two to three hours depending on whether the supply lines and drain also need updating to match the new piece.

Grapevine-Specific Factors

Under-sink shutoff valves in Grapevine homes built before the PEX era are frequently original steel or early plastic stop valves that have not been operated in years, and those valves corrode or seize shut from mineral buildup in exactly the water conditions this area has, which is why a straightforward faucet swap sometimes turns into also replacing a shutoff valve that will not close reliably once it is finally tested.

The occasional hard freeze that reaches North Texas most winters is the single biggest driver of outdoor hose bib repair calls in this service area, and a bib that survived several mild winters without issue can still fail the first time a genuinely hard freeze actually arrives, since the damage from freezing happens inside the pipe regardless of how many previous winters were too mild to cause it. Homes with an older, non-frost-proof bib are worth a proactive look before the next cold snap rather than waiting for a failure to show up as a wall leak the following spring.

What Happens On-Site

A technician identifies the fixture type and failure pattern first, drip location, valve type, or visible crack, since that determines the part needed before the truck is even opened. Most faucet, shower valve, and hose bib repairs are completed in a single visit with common parts carried on hand. A full sink or faucet installation includes checking the existing supply and drain connections before the new fixture is set, so a mismatch is caught at the estimate rather than mid-installation. Under-sink shutoff valves are tested as part of any fixture visit, since a seized valve found mid-repair is easier to address while the water is already off than to discover later.
