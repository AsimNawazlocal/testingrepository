Garbage Disposal Repair & Installation: What's Included

The most common disposal call is a unit that hums but will not turn. That almost always means the flywheel is jammed, a fruit pit, a piece of silverware, built-up fibrous waste, not a dead motor. Most units have a reset button on the underside for exactly this, and a hex-key wrench can often free the jam manually. A disposal that keeps jamming over and over is telling a different story: either the motor is on its way out, or something keeps going down it that should not.

A leak at the base is almost always the mounting flange or the seal where the unit meets the sink drain, not the motor housing. That is a straightforward reseal or flange swap, not a full unit replacement. A leak at the side, where the dishwasher drain line connects, is a separate and equally common failure: a loose hose clamp or a cracked fitting, easy to miss if the diagnosis stops at the first leak found.

Replacement units are sized by horsepower to match actual cooking habits. A light-use household does not need the motor a family running the disposal daily does. Installation also means matching the new unit's mounting system to the existing sink plumbing, which is not always a direct swap if the old unit used a different mount style than the new one ships with.

A disposal that trips the breaker repeatedly, rather than just humming or jamming, points to an electrical fault in the unit, not a mechanical jam. That changes the fix from a quick clear to a straightforward replacement, since a motor with an internal short is not a repairable part on most residential units.

A lingering odor even after running the disposal with water is usually trapped food debris in the splash-guard baffle or along the grind chamber, not a sign the unit needs replacing. That is a cleaning issue, not a mechanical one, and it gets checked before any repair is quoted so a household is not paying to replace a unit that only needed a proper clean-out.

Most jam-clearing calls run under an hour and under $150. A full replacement, including a mid-range half-horsepower unit and matching it to the existing sink mount, typically runs $300 to $500 installed, with a higher-horsepower unit for a larger household or heavier use running toward the upper end of that range.

Why This Looks Different Here

Grapevine's hard water plays a smaller role here than it does on water heaters or drains, but it is still a factor: mineral buildup around the disposal's splash guard and upper housing can make a unit feel sluggish well before the motor itself is actually failing, and a simple cleaning sometimes resolves a complaint that looked at first like it needed a replacement. That buildup is also why a disposal that has gone unused for an extended stretch, a vacation home or a rarely-used guest-suite kitchen, can seize up even without ever being overloaded, simply from mineral scale sitting against the flywheel undisturbed.

Kitchens remodeled in the last decade across Grapevine's newer subdivisions tend to have higher-horsepower units installed from the start, while older homes near Dove Road and College Street more often still have a builder-grade disposal from whenever the house was last updated, which is usually the unit replaced when a homeowner finally decides the noise or performance is no longer acceptable. Restaurant kitchens along Historic Main Street run commercial-grade disposals under far heavier daily use than any residential unit sees, and those units are serviced on a different schedule since downtime during business hours is a bigger cost than the repair itself.

The Visit, Step by Step

A technician first confirms whether the issue is the unit humming and jammed, silent and dead, or leaking, since each points to a different fix. A jam is typically cleared on the spot. A dead motor or a confirmed electrical fault moves straight to a replacement conversation, sized and quoted before the old unit comes out, with the new unit installed and tested under running water before the visit is finished. Most calls on this page, repair or replacement, are resolved in a single visit without needing a return trip for parts.
