What the work actually is

Leak work, drains, and pipe repair fill most of the week here. Pressure and scale are what make them happen, and winter is what turns ordinary wear into an emergency call.

Emergency plumbing

Active floods, major pipe bursts, and sewage backups get dispatched first, day or night. If the main shutoff isn't obvious, we walk you through finding it on the phone before anyone drives out.

24/7 dispatch

Water leak detection

Meter test with the house closed, then acoustic and infrared scanning to pinpoint a concealed leak before any wall or slab gets opened. On the bench the usual find is a pinhole in copper on a line that has been sitting at pressure it was never meant to hold.

Non-destructive scan

Whole-house repiping

Full replacement of failing copper or galvanized supply lines with PEX, usually the right call once a house is patching the same runs every winter. It is the highest-ticket job we do, and also the one that ends the repeat calls.

Copper & galvanized out, PEX in

Drain cleaning & clog removal

Cable for soft blockage and root intrusion, hydro jetting where the wall of the pipe has scaled over. Camera run afterward so the state of the line is something you look at, not something we tell you.

Camera shown on site

Water heater repair

Elements, thermostats, gas valves, anode rods. With water this hard, the rod and the sediment at the base of the tank are the first two things we check, not the last.

Same-day dispatch

Slab leak detection & repair

Warm spots on the floor, a water bill that jumped with no new fixture, or the sound of running water when every tap is off. Acoustic and thermal detection first, then a direct slab repair or an overhead PEX reroute.

Detect before we cut

Burst & broken pipe repair

Split hose bibs, a line run through an unheated garage, and the exposed section nobody thought about until a hard freeze. Emergency shutoff, the repair itself, and frost protection so it does not repeat next winter.

Winter emergency work

Gas line repair & leak detection

Pressure testing, emergency leak repairs, and appliance hookups for ranges, dryers, and water heaters. A suspected gas leak gets the same priority as an active flood.

Immediate 24/7

Water softener installation & repair

Loop and bypass plumbing for a softener, plus descaling the fixtures that have already suffered. We will tell you which of your symptoms a softener would actually have prevented and which it will not touch.

Toilet repair & installation

Flappers, fill valves, wax rings, and installing high-efficiency toilets. A toilet that runs on its own is almost always a flapper or fill-valve problem, not a sign of anything bigger.

4,980 ftSection 04, ground

Four things about this ground

Stepped elevation

Sandy climbs from the Jordan River valley floor at the western edge of the city up toward the mouth of Little Cottonwood Canyon in the east. A water system serving that much vertical drop is normally split into zones, each fed from its own tank, which is why pressure and fixture wear can genuinely differ between houses just a few streets apart.

Hard water, real scale

Sandy's water tests at 9.1 grains per gallon, hard but not the valley's worst case (parts of Salt Lake City and Taylorsville test harder). It is still enough to crust aerators, seize cartridges in place, and shorten the life of elements sitting in the sediment at the base of a water heater tank.

Alluvial ground

Sandy's original townsite was platted on an alluvial terrace along the Jordan River drainage, built up over time by material washed down from the canyons. A trench here commonly runs into rounded cobble and gravel rather than clean soil, which makes excavation slower and makes proper bedding and backfill matter more than it would on a clay lot.

Two building eras

Sandy's historic core around Main and Center Streets dates to its 1870s founding as a silver-smelting and railroad town. Everything else is newer: the city's population jumped from 6,438 in 1970 to 52,210 by 1980 as pocket subdivisions were built out through the 1970s and 80s. Supply lines and drains age differently between the two, so the first question on the phone is usually what decade the house went up.

5,260 ftSection 06, questions

Questions we get from the bench

What should I do immediately during a plumbing emergency in Sandy?

First, locate and turn off your home's main water shutoff valve, typically near the water meter or where the main line enters the house. Then open a low-point faucet to relieve pressure and call a local 24/7 emergency plumber.

How do I keep my pipes from freezing during a Sandy cold snap?

Insulate exterior hose bibs, keep garage doors closed on lines that run through an unheated garage, and let interior faucets trickle on nights near or below freezing. If a pipe does freeze, shut off the main water valve immediately before attempting to thaw it.

What are the signs of a hidden slab leak in a Sandy home?

Key indicators include an unexplained jump in your water bill, warm spots on tile or flooring, the sound of running water when every tap is off, and hairline cracks in baseboards or the foundation slab. Acoustic leak detection is the reliable way to pinpoint the exact pipe location before any concrete is opened.

How long do water heaters typically last in Sandy, UT?

Standard tank water heaters generally last 8 to 12 years here, while well-maintained tankless units can run 18 to 25 years with annual descaling. A tank making rumbling noises or producing rusty hot water is usually past the point of a repair being worth it.

What does this cost?

Parts we recognize get a wide range on the phone. A fill valve, a frost-proof bib, or a water heater element are jobs we have priced many times over. Anything nobody has seen yet, including a leak behind drywall or a pressure complaint we have not gauged, gets no figure until someone is standing in front of it.
