About White City

White City is a small, fully independent city, not a Sandy neighborhood, though Sandy surrounds it on every side. It was founded in 1955 by M. Kenneth White, became a township in 2006, a metro township in 2017, and was formally incorporated as a city on May 1, 2024. At just under one square mile and a population of roughly 5,500, it's one of the smaller cities along the Wasatch Front.

White City's own water system

White City runs its own water utility, the White City Water Improvement District, separate from Sandy's municipal water system. Water hardness and mineral content can differ from what we see on Sandy's own system, so we test rather than assume when hard-water symptoms come up on a White City call.
