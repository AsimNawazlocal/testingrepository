Privacy Policy

This page explains what information this website and Sandy UT Plumbing Experts collect, how it's used, and who to contact with questions. Last updated October 2026.

Information we collect

This site does not use contact or quote-request forms. The only ways to reach us are by phone call or email, listed on the contact page. When you call, our dispatch team collects the information you choose to share, such as your name, address, and a description of the plumbing issue, in order to schedule a visit.

Like most websites, this site may use cookies or similar technology to understand general traffic patterns, such as which pages are visited and how people arrive at the site. This does not include payment information, since no payments or bookings are processed through the website itself.

How information is used

  • To schedule and dispatch a plumbing technician to the address you provide by phone.
  • To respond to questions sent by email or phone.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how visitors use this website, so it can be kept accurate and easy to use.

We do not sell personal information to third parties. Information shared with us by phone or email is used only to provide the plumbing service you're requesting, not for unrelated marketing purposes.

Third-party links

This site may link to third-party resources, such as manufacturer or utility websites referenced in our content. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites, and we encourage you to review their own policies.

Your choices

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Doing so may affect how some site features display, but it won't affect your ability to call or email us directly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in how the site operates. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact about this policy

Questions about this policy can be directed to contact@sandyutplumbingexperts.com or by calling (385) 555-0103.
